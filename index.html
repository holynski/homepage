About me

I'm a professor at Columbia University, and I lead a team at Google DeepMind working on generative 3D and world models. Previously, I was a postdoc at UC Berkeley with Alyosha Efros and Angjoo Kanazawa, and did my PhD at the University of Washington with Steve Seitz, Brian Curless, and Rick Szeliski.
